## Post-draft challenge

**Outcome: no blocker or important finding within the drafts' stated narrower scopes.** Exactly three is defensible here as an editorial structure, not a measured rank. The findings below do not establish that these are the uniquely best three choices for every reader.

### Exercise

The actual goal is “Maintain physical capacity through exercise”, and the title explicitly says “exercise foundations”. The draft matches all three blind exercise candidates. Its separate aerobic, strength and balance aims follow WHO guidance, while noting that activities can train more than one aim. That is useful overlap rather than padding: walking does not automatically cover deliberate strengthening or balance practice.

Nutrition is not an important omission under this narrower question: its exclusion is recorded and broader independence factors are acknowledged. Flexibility can matter for individual restrictions but is not established as a universal fourth equivalent foundation. The ability/condition caveat and unsteadiness warning address the principal applicability concern. The draft avoids promising prevention of all falls or illness. There is no unsupported comparative ranking.

### Learning

The blind set grouped feedback with retrieval and made initial understanding a separate foundation. The draft instead offers retrieval, spacing and correction. This would be a weak claim to three independent leading memory techniques, because the third recommendation partly completes the first. The initial title “Three ways to remember what you study” was therefore a minor framing concern.

The inspected final title, “A three-step routine for remembering what you study”, and introduction explicitly describe connected steps. The description, feedback detail and limitations also disclose the relationship. This resolves that minor concern: a useful routine may separate a safeguard into its own action without claiming an independent benefit of equal size. The numbered order should not be read as a strict execution order; correction follows an attempt within each spaced session, as the copy explains.

Initial learning/understanding remains necessary, but the audience is remembering already studied material and the limitation explicitly says recall cannot replace learning it first. Its omission as a separate recommendation is therefore not important for this article. Explaining an idea supplies a conceptual retrieval example; the draft does not restrict retrieval to isolated factual flashcards. No measured optimal schedule or universal ranking is claimed. No outstanding blocker, important finding or required fourth recommendation was identified.

### Finance

The actual goal is “Reduce vulnerability to everyday financial shocks”, narrower than general financial security. Cash-flow timing, accessible reserves and costly borrowing are plausible complementary foundations for that goal. The article does not imply that a calendar itself creates income, or that everyone can save through discipline. It identifies US/UK source assumptions, international differences and consequential debt priorities. Insurance and long-term saving are acknowledged rather than presented as unnecessary.

The blind set placed active improvement of the income–cost margin ahead of simple awareness. The draft's shortfall and low-income caveats appropriately limit its promise, so this is not an important omission under the narrower shock-focused goal. **Optional minor improvement:** an actionable sentence directing a reader with an ongoing essential-income shortfall towards local benefits/support and feasible income or essential-cost changes would make the exception more useful. Arrears/debt advice already supplies a concrete route for readers in repayment difficulty.

Savings and debt reduction both absorb shocks and compete for available money. The article presents conditional foundations, not a universal allocation sequence, and protects essentials before debt optimisation. A standalone ranking of saving versus debt repayment would require a more explicit decision framework; the current wording does not claim one. No unsupported universal top-three promise was found.
